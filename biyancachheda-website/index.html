Author. Founder. Always curious.

I'm a Grade 12 student in Mumbai who loves turning ideas into something real. At 15, I wrote Magical Moments, a collection of children's stories. In Grade 11, I started EduAid, an initiative that introduces children to financial literacy and supports access to educational resources.

The two projects look different, but both grew from something I care about: making knowledge accessible, whether through a story a child can relate to or a lesson that helps them understand money a little better.

Away from my projects, you'll usually find me at the piano, on a pickleball court, out for a drive with friends and family, listening to music or working out at the gym. I love being around people, hearing their stories, and discovering something new along the way.

The book

Magical Moments

Nine stories. One idea brought to life.

I wrote it at 15 and published it at 16: a collection of nine illustrated stories for young readers.

Talking trees, a sleepy sun, a brave turtle and a flying fish: each story carries a small lesson about caring for nature, teamwork, honesty, imagination and believing in yourself.

Since its release in early 2026 it has sold more than 350 copies across 23 Indian states, and all of the proceeds have been donated.

EduAid

EduAid: building brighter futures.

Financial literacy, one lesson at a time.

I started EduAid in Grade 11 with the belief that children should have opportunities to learn skills they can use throughout their lives.

15

Students taught

Through Money Matters, EduAid's online classes.

90+

Children reached

Children received supplies through EduAid donation drives in Mumbai.

Through Money Matters, a course I developed and teach online, I have introduced young learners to the basics of money: budgeting, saving, distinguishing needs from wants, understanding interest and making thoughtful financial decisions.

I wanted the lessons to feel practical rather than intimidating. Money is something children encounter in everyday life, whether they're deciding how to use pocket money or thinking about saving for something they want. My aim was always to help them understand those decisions and feel more confident asking questions.

Beyond the classroom, I lead EduAid's Instagram advocacy, sharing financial literacy with a wider audience.

Beyond

Off the page

Not everything I enjoy needs to become a project. Some of my favourite parts of life happen away from a screen, a notebook or a to-do list.

Piano

Piano gives me a reason to slow down and focus on one thing at a time. I started learning in sixth grade and returned to it because I enjoy having something creative that belongs just to me. Today I practise for 30 to 60 minutes a day, working on more complex pieces. In Grade 9, I was the pianist for my school's Independence Day function.

Family

Every day I spend one to two hours caring for my grandparents, who have mobility needs. I help with everyday tasks and keep them company. Sometimes that means playing a song for them, like in this video with my dadi and dadu.

Drives

Some of my favourite time with my dadi and dadu is spent on our drives around Mumbai, talking and watching the city go by.

Golf

Golf is quieter and slower than anything else I play, and it is all about patience. I'm still learning, and the simulator lets me take one shot at a time, see what went wrong and try again.
